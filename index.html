Hello, I'm Aleksey Shirokov. I started Dupli Espreso as a workshop for small, useful tools designed to accompany people in their everyday lives. Each app is made with craftsmanship, human-centered design, and modern simplicity

01 / The name

I believe good things come from caring about the details.

Whether it’s a cup of espresso or a piece of software, the difference lies in how thoughtfully it’s made.

Espresso is a simple drink. Almost any café can make one. But what about a perfectly balanced cup, full of flavour, that brightens your day and fills it with joy?

That takes craft behind the scenes: selecting the right coffee beans, carefully roasting them, and precisely balancing every variable — the coffee-to-water ratio, grind size, brewing time, and temperature.

02 / The craft

It’s the same with apps.

Each of my applications is the result of extensive research, meticulous attention to detail, fine-tuning, and a continuous effort to improve the user experience.
